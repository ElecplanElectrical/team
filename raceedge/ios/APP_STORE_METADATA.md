## Description
RACEEDGE gives Australian racing fans a focused view of today's racing, live race information and analytical selections.

Built for fast race-day use, RACEEDGE brings together meeting information, race fields, current market prices, scratchings and RaceEdge analysis in one place.

Features include:
• Australian thoroughbred racing coverage
• Greyhound racing coverage
• Live meetings and race fields
• Current scratchings and race changes
• RaceEdge selections and analysis
• Runner details and market information
• Recorded results and performance summaries
• Pull-to-refresh race-day updates

RaceEdge ratings and selections are analytical estimates based on available racing data. They are not guaranteed outcomes.

RACEEDGE does not place bets or process wagering transactions.

## Review notes
RACEEDGE is a racing information and analytics product. It does not accept wagers, process deposits or withdrawals, or place bets on behalf of users.

The app reads racing information from the RaceEdge backend. Provider credentials remain server-side and are not included in the iOS application.

For review, launch the app with an internet connection. The Home and Races tabs load live race-day information from the production RaceEdge API. The Tips and Results tabs show RaceEdge analytical selections and recorded performance data when available.

No user account is required for version 1.0.

## App Privacy working notes
Current iOS client does not intentionally collect user account data, contacts, precise location, health information, financial information, advertising identifiers or tracking data.

Before submission, answer the App Store Connect privacy questionnaire against the final production build and server logging/analytics configuration.
