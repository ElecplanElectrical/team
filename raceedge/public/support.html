RACEEDGE Support

RACEEDGE provides Australian racing information, live race data and analytical selections.

App issues

If live racing data is temporarily unavailable, pull down to refresh and check your internet connection. If the issue continues, record the time, meeting and race number involved so it can be investigated.

Feedback

Feedback on race coverage, fields, scratchings, results or app behaviour is welcome. Include the device model, iOS version and a short description of what happened.

Important

RACEEDGE does not place bets or process wagering transactions. RaceEdge ratings and selections are analytical estimates and are not guaranteed outcomes.
