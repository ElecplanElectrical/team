RACEEDGE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

RACEEDGE is an Australian racing information and analytics application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the RACEEDGE app and services.

Information we collect

Version 1.0 of the RACEEDGE iOS app does not require a user account and is not designed to collect your name, contacts, precise location, health information, financial account information or advertising identifiers.

Technical information

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process limited technical information required to operate and secure the service, such as network requests, timestamps, IP addresses, device/browser technical information and service logs. This information is used for security, reliability, diagnostics and abuse prevention.

Racing data

The app retrieves racing information and analytical outputs from the RACEEDGE backend. Racing-provider credentials are stored server-side and are not embedded in the iOS app.

Tracking and advertising

RACEEDGE version 1.0 does not use advertising tracking or cross-app tracking.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers where necessary to operate, secure and maintain RACEEDGE, or where required by law.

Data retention

Operational logs may be retained for a limited period where necessary for security, diagnostics, legal compliance and service reliability.

Children

RACEEDGE is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy as RACEEDGE develops. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the RACEEDGE operator using the support details published on the RACEEDGE support page.
